Good to know

A few gentle questions

Do I need to speak French already?

Not at all. Wherever you are today — a few words, or comfortable conversation — we begin exactly there, and move gently at your own pace.

How does the coaching work?

We meet one to one, online, from wherever you are in the world. Each conversation lasts fifty minutes and belongs entirely to you.

Can you be with me while I'm in France?

Yes — it's the heart of During France. Whenever a day feels important, we find a quiet moment together, so you enjoy every part of your stay.

And after I return home?

That's Beyond France. We continue, gently and unhurried, so the country you love stays a warm part of your everyday life.

How do we begin?

With a calm, twenty-minute conversation. We say hello, I listen to the France you dream of, and together we find the way that feels right for you.
